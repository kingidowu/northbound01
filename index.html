Questions

The sponsorship questions, answered.

What fields and experience levels do you work with?

All of them. Whether you're early-career, mid-level, or senior - in tech, marketing, finance, healthcare, operations, design, or anything else - the assessment adapts to your situation and goals.

Do you guarantee a job?

No one honestly can. What we do is sharpen your resume, focus your search on the right roles, connect you with verified listings, and coach you through the process - which is what actually moves the odds in your favor.

Is the resume ATS check free?

Yes. Paste your resume (and optionally a job description) and get an instant score, missing keywords, and rewrite suggestions - no account needed.

Are the job listings legit?

Every listing is posted by a recruiter through our portal, and roles from vetted recruiters carry a ✓ Verified badge so you know who's behind them.

What happens after I submit the assessment?

You'll get an instant, personalized snapshot on screen, then we follow up by email with a tailored plan: target roles, positioning, and the next moves for your situation.
